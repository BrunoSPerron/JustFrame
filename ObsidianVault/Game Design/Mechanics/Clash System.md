# 🧠 Concept Overview

The **Clash System** activates when two attacks connect on the **same frame** or within a **tight clash window**. Rather than favoring one move, both characters **recoil** slightly in response, introducing a brief moment of tension.

This system does **not break the flow** of combat but instead creates a unique **micro-interaction** where both players have a chance to **regain advantage through timing and awareness**.

---

# ⚙️ Mechanic Details

### Trigger Condition

- A **Clash** occurs when:
    
    - Two attacks collide on the **exact same frame**
        
    - Or within a **small clash window** (tunable)
        

### Clash Result

- Both characters enter a **recoil state**:
    
    - **Short hit-pause** (brief freeze for feedback)
        
    - **Custom recoil animation** (character flinch or bounce)
        

### Recovery Priority

- During the recoil, The **first player** to input **Block (B)** recover immediately.
    
- The **faster responder** can reset to **neutral** or act offensively with **pressure or follow-up moves**
        

### Tie Condition

- If **both players input on the same frame**, they regain control **simultaneously**, resetting to neutral (or triggering a tied clash variant, if designed)
    

---

# 🎯 Design Intent

- Reward **precise timing** and **defensive reflexes**
    
- Add a layer of **interaction to simultaneous aggression** (e.g., both players attack on wake-up or during pressure breaks)
    
- Prevent **mindless button mashing** while still **rewarding commitment**
    
- Introduce **psychological mixups**—do you follow up, delay, or bait the clash response?
    
- Maintain **fluid combat pacing** while introducing **tactical pauses** for engagement
