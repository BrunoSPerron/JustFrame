A smug, airborne angel who hovers above the ground with disdain, punishing overreach with ruthless precision. He uses mid-height positioning and tight counter windows to humiliate opponents who dare challenge his timing or spacing.
- **Mobility:** Cannot run or crouch. Walks slowly with an air of superiority. Transitions into a floating Air Neutral stance using steps or jumps. Gains strong control over space with slow, deliberate air dashes and controlled double jumps.
- **Air Neutral:** His default state. Floats just above low attacks, changing how spacing and threat ranges work. Can transition fluidly between walk and hover, enabling position-based mind games.
- **Attack Height Shift:** Blocks mids with low guard, avoids lows entirely. In turn, his grounded attacks lose access to true lows and hit one level higher — creating a unique mix environment.
- Counterplay Threat: Tight, timing-based counters from Air Neutral slam opponents to the ground or bounce them upward. He thrives on punishing jump-ins and button mashing with brutal repositioning tools.
- **Throw Variety:** Throws are deliberate and humiliating. Includes air grabs, step-into-throw transitions, and passive counter throws that assert space. Low damage, but exceptional for knockdowns and control.
- **Clash / Recoil Response:** After a clash, can trigger a dismissive parry or poke, reinforcing his superiority and timing mastery.
- **Unique Move:** Judgment Drop — a delayed, descending kick that punishes impatience. On counter-hit, it bounces or slams the opponent depending on spacing.

**Note:** A position-dominant counter specialist who distorts traditional grounded play. Forces opponents to commit poorly, then punishes them with flair. Smug, efficient, and surgical in his rejection of mediocrity.
