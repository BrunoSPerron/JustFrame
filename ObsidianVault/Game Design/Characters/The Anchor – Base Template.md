# The Anchor – Base Template Character

## Overview

**Archetype:**  
- Brawler / Pugilist  
- Strong ground game and pressure  
- Balanced movement, average damage, no gimmicks  
- Default access to all core systems  

**Personality: Playful Technician**  
- A laid-back martial artist who smiles in the face of pressure and sees every fight as a mutual challenge. He fights cleanly, respects opponents, and enjoys solving them through skill—not domination. Calm and friendly, he values the process over the outcome, finding joy in every exchange.

**Style:** Karate-Inspired Martial Artist  
- Emphasis on timing, form, and technique. Punches, kicks, elbows. Low flash, high substance.  
- Influences: Kyokushin, Shotokan, with a hint of Wado-ryu for movement emphasis.
